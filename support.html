Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Tap a column to drop the next tile. Matching tiles merge, and a merge can set off a chain reaction. Fill your Codex by forging new worlds, and spend a Surge when the board gets tight to clear the lowest tiles and keep going.

Does my game save? Can I take a break?

Yes. Your board saves automatically after every move and resumes right where you left off the next time you open the app, same day or later.

I bought Tumbry+ but don't see it on a new device.

Open Collections or Stats from the bottom bar, tap Unlock Tumbry+, then Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on, it will unlock at no charge. Tumbry+ is a one-time purchase, so there is no subscription.

What does Tumbry+ include?

Four more collections to complete (The Garden, The Forge, The Abyss, and The Foundry), each with its own board, plus an Advanced Stats screen that tracks your progress across every collection. One time, yours forever.

Is my progress backed up or synced between devices?

Not yet. Your progress is saved on your device only, so deleting the app or switching devices starts you fresh (your Tumbry+ purchase can always be restored). We're looking at cloud sync for a future update.

Does Tumbry work offline? Are there ads?

Tumbry is fully playable offline and contains no ads and no timers.

How do I switch collections?

Open Collections from the bottom bar and choose one. Each collection keeps its own board, so you can switch any time and pick up right where you left off in each.
