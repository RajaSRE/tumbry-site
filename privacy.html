Privacy Policy

Tumbry · Maran Studios · Last updated: August 24, 2026

Tumbry does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. This page explains that in full.

Information we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or any usage or analytics data. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.

Information stored on your device

Tumbry saves your game progress (scores, statistics, your collection and codex progress, your current board, your selected collection, and minor app settings) locally on your device, in the app's own private storage. This information never leaves your device. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and it is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Tumbry offers a single optional one-time purchase, Tumbry+. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive or see your payment details or your Apple Account. The app only knows whether Tumbry+ has been unlocked on your device. You can restore a previous purchase, or view and manage purchases, through Apple. Apple's handling of purchases is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any.

Children's privacy

Tumbry is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the app does not knowingly gather information from children under 13 (or the relevant age in your region).

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and revise the date above. Because the app collects no data, we do not expect material changes.
