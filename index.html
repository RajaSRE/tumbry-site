Tumbry

Drop. Merge. Make a universe.

A merge puzzle for iPhone where one drop can set off a chain reaction across the whole board, and every tile you make is a new world to collect.

Free · No ads · No timers · Plays offline

How it plays

Tap a column to drop a tile. Matching tiles merge, and a merge can trigger the next, and the next, in a chain reaction that blooms across the board. Every tile you forge is a world: stardust becomes rock, rock becomes planets, planets ignite into stars, stars collapse into galaxies, black holes, and quasars.

  • One endless board: open the app and you're already playing.
  • Satisfying chain-reaction combos with a real sense of payoff.
  • A Codex of worlds to discover and complete.
  • When the board gets tight, spend a Surge to clear space and keep going.
  • Your run saves automatically and resumes anytime.
  • Every world is drawn in code, so the whole game is a tiny download.

Questions

Is Tumbry free?

Yes. The full game with The Cosmos collection is free. Tumbry+ is an optional one-time purchase ($2.99 in the US) that adds four more collections (The Garden, The Forge, The Abyss and The Foundry) plus Advanced Stats. No subscription.

Are there ads?

No. No ads, no timers, no energy meters, and nothing that makes you wait.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Tumbry needs no account and no internet connection to play.

Will I lose my board if I close the app?

No. Your board saves after every move and is waiting exactly where you left it. Each collection keeps its own board, so switching never costs you a run.

How does a run end?

When the board is full and your Surge meter isn't charged to clear it. Surge recharges as you merge, so a tight board isn't the end. You get a summary of your run, and a fresh board is one tap away.

Which devices does it run on?

Tumbry is built for iPhone and needs iOS 17 or later.

I bought Tumbry+ on another iPhone. How do I get it back?

Open Collections or Stats, then tap Restore Purchases on the Tumbry+ screen.
