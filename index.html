Tumbry

A cosmic merge puzzle.

Drop a tile into any column. Matching tiles merge, and a merge can trigger the next, and the next, in a chain reaction that blooms across the board. Every tile you forge is a world: stardust becomes rock, rock becomes planets, planets ignite into stars, stars collapse into galaxies, black holes, and quasars.

  • One endless board: open the app and you're already playing.
  • Satisfying chain-reaction combos with a real sense of payoff.
  • A Codex of worlds to discover and complete.
  • Your run saves automatically and resumes anytime.
  • No ads. No tracking. Works offline, and your data never leaves your device.
